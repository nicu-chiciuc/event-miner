Event Miner experiments

Time runs slower the deeper you are. Each experiment tests one idea about that. Play each for ten minutes and note which one you want more of.

  1. Dive: Waves
    Dome Keeper with a time well. Waves hit the base on the surface clock, so every deep minute costs many surface minutes.
  2. Dive: Years
    The field is steep. A long deep dive skips years while the colony above eats, grows and researches.
  3. Sand
    A falling-sand world where every row runs on its own clock. Dig down and watch the world above speed up.
  4. Chain
    Vertical production puzzles. The depth you watch from sets the game speed. Place each machine where its clock suits it.
  5. Original prototype
    The first Phaser version, for comparison.

Every experiment accepts ?law=exp|linear|step and ?double=<metres> to try other time laws.
